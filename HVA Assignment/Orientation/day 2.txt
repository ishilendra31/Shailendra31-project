3.☕ Skim v/s Deep Reading Challenge:
Let’s test two reading styles. For the first paragraph, just skim to get the idea. For the second, read slowly and try to understand it.
Paragraph 1 (Skim):
Coffee is a popular drink made from roasted beans. People enjoy it hot or cold, and it can be found in almost every country. It helps people feel more awake and is often consumed in the morning or during work. There are many types of coffee drinks, like espresso, latte, and cappuccino.
Paragraph 2 (Deep Read):
Coffee doesn’t just wake you up — it actually changes how your brain works. Inside your brain is a chemical called adenosine that makes you feel sleepy. Caffeine blocks this chemical, so you feel more alert. It also boosts other brain signals that help you focus. But too much coffee can mess with your sleep and make you depend on it every day. So, it works best when used wisely.

Summarise both the passages in 1-2 lines

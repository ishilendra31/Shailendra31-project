
1.What is the primary focus of Hyperverge Academy?

The primary focus of HyperVerge Academy is to provide young people from under-resourced communities with access to skilling and networking opportunities. The goal is to help them secure credible , well-paying jobs in the technology sector.


2.You are given a statement. State whether it is True or False.Mentors are here to teach you and give lectures.

False, The program follows a self-learning approach where students are responsible for their own learning. Mentors provide guidance, feedback, and support when students proactively reach out with questions.This means that learning is the student’s responsibility, and they are expected to take ownership by asking questions and engaging with mentors instead of relying on them for direct instruction.


3.What is the role of a mentor?

The role of a mentor is to provide guidance, support, and progress tracking. Mentors help students solve their doubts related to learning and projects and They provide insights and suggestions to help students improve , they monitor students learning journey and growth .Since this is a self-learning program, students must take initiative to ask questions and seek feedback .Mentors are volunteers with industry experience who assist students, but learning remains the student’s responsibility.


4.How much time should full-time learners spend on the program?

Full-time students in the program are expected to dedicate 8 hours per day to their learning.


5.How much time should part-time learners spend on the program?

Part-time learners such as college students are expected to commit 3 to 4 hours daily to the program to ensure steady progress.


6.What are the 5 main Expectations we have at Hyperverge Academy?

The five main expectations at HyperVerge Academy are:
Consistency - Learners should show up regularly, stay committed, and maintain steady progress throughout the program. 
2.Ownership – This is self- learning program, Mentors are not teaching you. They are not giving lectures so take responsibility for your learning journey. Be proactive in solving problems, seeking help when needed, and making the most of available resources. 
3.Respond – Actively engage in discussions, respond to feedback, and communicate effectively with mentors and peers to enhance the learning experience. 
4.Effort – Put in your best effort daily. Whether it’s completing assignments, practicing skills, or participating in activities, continuous effort is crucial for success. 
5.Leave – It is mandatory to apply for leave through the following process and leaves will be approved by the program team based in a case by case basis.


7.What does the R stand for in the COREL acronym?

The R in the COREL acronym stands for "respond".


8.What do you understand by ownership?

Ownership means taking full responsibility for your learning in the program. Since this is a self-learning environment, mentors are not there to teach or give lectures. Instead, you must take the initiative to ask questions and seek feedback.


9.If we find that you are not performing sincerely, what will happen?

If I am not performing sincerely and not putting in my genuine effort, I will receive a warning from the team. They care about my journey and how I get there, so if I don’t put in the work, it will be noticed.


10.What will ensure that you end up getting a job?

To ensure that I end up getting a job, I need to fully commit to the program by following the COREL principles:
1.Consistency – Success requires discipline. I must show up regularly, stay engaged, and actively participate in the program.
2.Ownership – My learning is my responsibility. This is a self-learning program, meaning mentors will not teach me everything step by step. I need to take initiative, ask questions, seek feedback, and push myself to improve. The more proactive I am, the more I will learn and grow. 
3.Respond & Reach Out – Communication is key. I must not hesitate to ask questions, share concerns, and interact with my mentors and peers. 4.Effort – My results will reflect the effort I put in. If I study well, complete tasks, and stay dedicated, my progress will be noticed. The program values my journey, not just the final outcome, so I must stay committed and work hard.
4.Leaves – The program allows a limited number of leaves. I must plan them wisely to avoid unnecessary absences that could affect my learning and performance.

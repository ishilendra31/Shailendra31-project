I learned that finishing a task quickly with outside help, like ChatGPT or ready-made answers, may seem useful, but it does not help real learning. It hides struggles and gives a false feeling of knowing something. Good coders take their time, avoid shortcuts, and think deeply to solve problems. HVA encourages learning by facing challenges, asking for help in the right way, and growing through thinking. In the end, true success comes from understanding, not just getting things done fast.

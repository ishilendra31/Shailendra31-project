Reflection on the HVA Mentorship Program & My Plan for Growth
The HVA Mentorship Program is designed to help students from different backgrounds develop technical skills, improve problem-solving abilities, and secure jobs in 
the tech industry. It promotes self-learning, where we take responsibility for our own progress while receiving guidance and support from mentors. This program 
strongly aligns with my vision because I always want to become a skilled programmer and build a successful career in technology. I have always believed that hard 
work and consistency lead to success, and HVA’s focus on deep learning rather than just quick solutions will help me build a strong foundation in programming, 
which will be valuable in the long run.Through this program, I expect a well-structured learning process that helps me develop logical thinking and problem-solving 
skills step by step. I also look forward to being part of a community where I can discuss doubts, share knowledge, and learn from others' experiences. Moreover, I 
hope the program will encourage me to think and solve problems independently rather than just memorizing concepts. At the same time, I have certain expectations from 
myself to make the most of this journey. I aim to stay consistent by learning something new every day, no matter how small the step. I will make sure to ask for help 
when needed instead of struggling alone, as reaching out to mentor will accelerate my learning. Additionally, I plan to engage more in discussions to gain confidence 
and improve my communication skills. Instead of taking shortcuts, I will focus on understanding concepts deeply to become a better problem solver.To maximize my 
growth and make the most of this program, I plan to stick to a learning routine. Setting aside time daily for coding, revising past concepts, and practicing 
problem-solving will help me stay consistent. I will also focus on improving my problem-solving skills by breaking down problems into smaller steps, applying dry 
running techniques, and understanding the logic behind different challenges. Actively participating in group discussions will allow me to build confidence in 
explaining concepts.Improving my focus and time management is another priority, as I sometimes struggle with distractions. To overcome this, I will follow a daily 
timetable and take short breaks to maintain concentration. Learning from my mistakes is a part of my plan I will view errors as opportunities to grow, and debugging 
will help me improve my coding skills. Lastly, I will set small goals and track my progress, such as mastering a specific concept or solving a set number of problems 
each week. By following these steps , I believe I can make the most of the HVA program and continue growing as a skilled programmer. The journey has just begun, 
and I am excited to keep learning, improving, and building my future in the tech industry
